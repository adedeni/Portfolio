FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast is delivery?

Most top-ups arrive within seconds of payment. You get a receipt for every order.

Which networks do you support?

MTN, Airtel and 9mobile for airtime and data, plus electricity tokens and cable TV renewals.

How do I fund my wallet?

By bank transfer or card. Your balance is ready to use as soon as the payment clears.

What if a top-up fails?

Failed orders are refunded to your wallet automatically, and our support team is available around the clock.
