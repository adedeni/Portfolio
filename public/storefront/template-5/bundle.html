FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast is delivery?

Most orders arrive within seconds of payment, and you get a receipt for every one.

Which networks and billers do you support?

MTN, Airtel and 9mobile for airtime and data, plus electricity tokens, DSTV and Spectranet.

How do I fund my wallet?

By bank transfer or card. Your balance is ready to use as soon as the payment clears.

What happens if a top-up fails?

Failed orders are refunded to your wallet automatically. If anything looks wrong, our support team will help.

Can I resell airtime and data?

Yes. Resellers get discounted rates, their own dashboard and an API for their website or app.
